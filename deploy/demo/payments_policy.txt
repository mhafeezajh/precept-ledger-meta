HARBOR FEDERAL SAVINGS BANK (FICTIONAL, FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY)
Payments Operations Policy for Automated Agents
Policy PO-7, version 3. Owner: Compliance.

1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to every software agent that acts for Payments Operations, including
the agent named payments-ops.

1.2 An action that this policy does not permit is not allowed.

2. Vendor information

2.1 The payments-ops agent may look up vendor records without approval.

3. Outgoing payments

3.1 The payments-ops agent may pay a known payee up to $5,000 per payment without approval.

3.2 Any payment over $5,000 requires approval by a payments supervisor before it is sent.

3.3 Any payment to a payee that has not been paid before requires approval by a payments
supervisor, whatever the amount.

3.4 Payments must be made in U.S. dollars (USD). A payment in any other currency is not
allowed.

3.5 No single payment may exceed $250,000. Larger payments are made by staff, outside the
agent.

4. Email

4.1 The payments-ops agent may send email to bank staff without approval.

4.2 Any email to a recipient outside the bank requires approval by a payments supervisor.

5. Business hours

5.1 Business hours are 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, bank time (New York).
